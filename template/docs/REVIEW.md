# Review

An independent pass over a full render: a fresh agent, or the lead in a separate pass that has not just built the
shots. Look at the cue sheets (`node tools/cues_sheet.mjs`), the review sheets of the render, the check reports, and
listen. Write every problem here first, then fix in order, then re-run the checks. Lyric lines by number only.

- **P0** wrong picture for its line · wrong time (a cut or hit off its cue) · a check that fails · a page error ·
  a rule of BRIEF.md broken · a seam or transition that breaks
- **P1** not refined: weak composition, a still or floating picture, unreadable type, a flat colour step, a lazy
  transition
- **P2** nice to have
